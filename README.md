# Done By Morning 🌙

AI-powered overnight research and analysis service.

**Tagline**: Submit at 6pm. Wake up to answers.

## Concept

Clients submit research requests by 6pm, receive comprehensive reports by 6am the next morning. AI agents work through the night analyzing sources and synthesizing insights.

## Services

1. **Market Research** - TAM/SAM/SOM, trends, growth drivers
2. **Competitor Analysis** - Pricing, features, SWOT, gaps
3. **Business Plans** - Full investor-ready plans
4. **Due Diligence** - M&A and investment analysis
5. **Industry Reports** - Deep dives on sectors
6. **Go-To-Market Strategy** - Launch plans and positioning

## Go-To-Market

### Phase 1: Founder-led sales (Month 1-3)
- LinkedIn outreach to VCs, startup founders, strategy consultants
- 5 free reports to build testimonials
- Case studies from each engagement

### Phase 2: Content marketing (Month 4-6)
- Publish sample reports (anonymized)
- SEO for "overnight research", "fast market research"
- Newsletter with industry insights

### Phase 3: Partnerships (Month 7-12)
- White-label for consulting firms
- Integrations with VC deal flow tools
- Referral program (20% commission)
