FAQ

Questions? We've Got Answers.

How does the overnight research work?

A research agent breaks your question into focused sub-questions, runs web searches for each, and has a large language model (Anthropic Claude by default) write a report using only the sources it found. Every claim carries a numbered citation. The engine is open source on GitHub.

How good is the research?

It is as good as the public sources available on your topic, and every source is listed so you can check it. It works best for market scans, competitor overviews, and background research. It is not legal, financial, or investment advice.

What if I need revisions?

Revisions are included. Focused reports get 1 round, Comprehensive gets unlimited revisions for 48 hours, and Enterprise gets 7 days.

Is my information confidential?

Your brief is used only to produce your report and is never published. If your question involves sensitive details, mention it in the form and we will agree on how to handle them before you share anything confidential.

What if I submit after 6pm?

Requests are accepted any time. Briefs received by 6pm (US Eastern) are worked on that night; later briefs go into the next night's batch.

How do I pay?

Nothing is charged on this site. After you send a brief we reply by email to confirm scope, price, and delivery time, and work starts once you approve.
