Questions? We've Got Answers.

How do you deliver such comprehensive research overnight?

We use advanced AI agents that can analyze hundreds of sources simultaneously. While you sleep, our systems are synthesizing data from public filings, industry reports, news articles, and proprietary databases into actionable insights.

What's the quality compared to consulting firms?

Our reports are comprehensive and well-sourced. For most business decisions, they're comparable to what you'd get from a mid-tier consulting engagement. For highly specialized or regulated industries, we recommend supplementing with domain experts.

What if I need revisions?

All plans include revisions. Focused reports get 1 round, Comprehensive gets unlimited revisions for 48 hours, and Enterprise gets 7 days. Most clients don't need revisions, but we've got you covered if you do.

Is my information confidential?

Absolutely. We sign NDAs on request, and all client data is encrypted and deleted after 30 days. We never share client briefs or use them to train our systems.

What if I submit after 6pm?

We accept requests 24/7. Submit by 6pm local time for next-morning delivery. Requests after 6pm are delivered by 6pm the following day (still ~24 hours).

Do you offer refunds?

Yes. If your report doesn't meet your expectations after revisions, we offer a full refund. No questions asked. We've issued less than 1% refunds to date.
